Word NYC

Put your show on the map

Word NYC shows New Yorkers what's playing tonight, from jazz clubs and indie rooms to the Met and Carnegie Hall. People open it when they're deciding where to go in the next few hours.

What a sponsored listing gets

  • Your show at the top of the list on the nights you choose, labeled Sponsored.
  • A highlighted pin on the map, drawn above the others.
  • A place in the story graphic people share to Instagram and other apps.
  • Your own ticket link, show photo, program and description in the show's details.

Who it's for

Venues, promoters, festivals, ensembles and artists with a show in New York City.
