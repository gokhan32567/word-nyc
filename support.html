Help

Word NYC maps tonight's live music in New York. The lineup updates every morning at 5 AM.

Noon notifications

Tap Turn on under "Tonight's picks at noon" on the map. You get one notification a day around noon with a few of the night's best shows. To stop them, open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Word NYC, or turn them off in your browser's site settings.

Following venues

Tap the heart next to a venue, in a show's details or on the venue's page. When a venue you follow has a show, the noon notification mentions it. Follows are saved on your phone; there's no account. Notifications mention your five most recent follows.

A show is missing or wrong

Venues change their calendars often. Always check the venue's own listing before you go. To add or correct a show, use Submit a show.

Tickets

Word NYC doesn't sell tickets. "Get tickets" opens the venue's or ticket seller's page, and your purchase is between you and them. Some links earn Word NYC a commission at no extra cost to you.
