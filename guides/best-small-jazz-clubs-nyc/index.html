The best small jazz clubs in NYC

Big halls are great, but jazz in New York lives in small rooms: basements in the Village, neighborhood bars in Brooklyn, a brownstone in Harlem. Here are the ones worth knowing, each linked to what it has on tonight.

Village Vanguard

Greenwich Village, Manhattan · 178 7th Ave S

A wedge-shaped basement room on Seventh Avenue South, open since 1935 and one of the most famous jazz clubs in the world. Many classic live albums were recorded here. Engagements usually run a week, with two sets a night, and the Vanguard Jazz Orchestra plays Mondays. Tonight: 2 shows.

Smalls Jazz Club

Greenwich Village, Manhattan · 183 W 10th St

A narrow basement club on West 10th Street in the West Village, known for straight-ahead jazz, late sets and jam sessions that run past midnight. Its sister room Mezzrow is a few doors down. Tonight: 6 shows.

Mezzrow

Greenwich Village, Manhattan · 163 W 10th St

A small, quiet listening room on West 10th Street, a few doors from Smalls, built around piano, duos and trios. Tonight: 5 shows.

Smoke Jazz Club

Upper West Side, Manhattan · 2751 Broadway

An intimate Upper West Side supper club on Broadway, with dinner seating and nightly sets from established players. Tonight: 3 shows.

Zinc Bar

Greenwich Village, Manhattan · 82 W 3rd St

A Greenwich Village basement bar on West 3rd Street with jazz, Latin and Brazilian music most nights. Tonight: 1 show.

Arthur's Tavern

Greenwich Village, Manhattan · 57 Grove St

A long-running West Village bar on Grove Street with live music every night, from jazz to blues and Dixieland. Tonight: 4 shows.

Cellar Dog

Greenwich Village, Manhattan · 75 Christopher St

A West Village basement bar on Christopher Street with pool tables, games and live jazz most evenings.

The Jazz Gallery

Chelsea, Manhattan · 1158 Broadway

A nonprofit performance space on Broadway known for championing new music and emerging jazz artists. Tonight: 2 shows.

Close Up

Lower East Side, Manhattan · 154 Orchard St

A small Lower East Side listening room on Orchard Street with jazz most nights. Tonight: 3 shows.

Bill's Place

Harlem, Manhattan · 148 W 133rd St

A small Harlem room on West 133rd Street, the block once known as Swing Street, where saxophonist Bill Saxton leads weekend sets.

Bar LunÀtico

Bed-Stuy, Brooklyn · 486 Halsey St

A small Bed-Stuy bar on Halsey Street with music most nights: jazz, folk, Brazilian and more, in a room where people actually listen. Tonight: 2 shows.

Ornithology Jazz Club

Bushwick, Brooklyn · 6 Suydam St

A Bushwick jazz bar on Suydam Street with nightly sets and sessions from younger players. Tonight: 2 shows.

Bar Bayeux

Flatbush, Brooklyn · 1066 Nostrand Ave

A cozy jazz bar on Nostrand Avenue in Prospect Lefferts Gardens with live sets most nights. Tonight: 2 shows.

Soapbox Gallery

Prospect Heights, Brooklyn · 636 Dean St

A Prospect Heights listening room on Dean Street presenting jazz and improvised music in a gallery setting.
