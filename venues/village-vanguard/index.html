Village Vanguard

178 7th Ave S, New York, NY 10014 · Greenwich Village · Venue website · More Jazz tonight

A wedge-shaped basement room on Seventh Avenue South, open since 1935 and one of the most famous jazz clubs in the world. Classic live albums by Sonny Rollins, John Coltrane and Bill Evans were recorded here. Engagements usually run six nights, and the Vanguard Jazz Orchestra has played Monday nights since 1966.
