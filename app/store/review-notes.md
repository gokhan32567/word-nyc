# App Review information

Paste into App Store Connect → version page → App Review Information.

- **Sign-in required:** No (the app has no accounts).
- **Contact:** your name, phone and email (Apple calls only if something's unclear).
- **Notes:**

> Word NYC is a guide to tonight's live music in New York City. A new lineup of 100+ shows from 150+ venues is
> built every morning; the app shows it on a map and a list that update through the night (live / starting
> soon / later).
>
> Native features in this build:
> • Push notifications: an opt-in "Tonight's picks" notification once a day at noon (tap "Turn on" under the
>   list). Tapping the notification opens that show's details. It can be turned off inside the app (link at the
>   bottom of the list) or in Settings.
> • Follow venues: tap the heart on a show's details or on a venue page. Followed venues are saved on the device,
>   marked in the list, and mentioned in the noon notification when they have a show.
> • Offline: the app keeps the latest lineup on the device and lists it when there's no connection.
> • iOS share sheet for shows and for a story image of tonight's map; Apple Maps for directions; haptics.
>
> Tickets: Word NYC doesn't sell anything. "Get tickets" opens the venue's or ticket seller's own page in
> Safari's in-app view, to buy tickets for real-world concerts (guideline 3.1.3(e) / 3.1.5). Some of these
> links are affiliate links, disclosed under the button ("may earn a commission").
>
> Sponsored listings: venues pay Word NYC directly (outside the app) to feature a show. Featured shows and
> notifications are always labeled "Sponsored". The notification opt-in text says it may be led by a sponsored
> show.
>
> No ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases.

## If Apple pushes back

- **4.2 Minimum functionality ("just a website"):** point to the native features above. The next native steps,
  if needed: a home-screen widget with tonight's picks, and Live Activities for a show you're heading to.
- **4.5.4 Push for marketing:** the opt-in text mentions sponsored shows, and the in-app "Turn off" link is
  at the bottom of the list (shown when notifications are on).
